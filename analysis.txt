JOB SCRAPER AUTOMATION AUDIT (2026-05-01)

Scope
- Repo: /home/runner/work/afidiOS-finder/afidiOS-finder
- Workflows reviewed: .github/workflows/daily-scrape.yml, daily-tutoring.yml
- Pipelines reviewed: scheduled_scraper_v3.py (general), tutoring_scraper.py (tutoring)
- Core components: site_scrapers.py, semantic_analyzer.py, incremental_scraper.py,
  tutoring_helpers.py, tutoring_classifier.py, job_exporter.py, job_helpers.py

1) Pipeline over-triggering (3 runs/day)
Findings
- daily-scrape.yml defines TWO cron schedules:
  - 0 6 * * * (UTC)
  - 0 5 * * * (UTC)
  These are intended to cover DST, but they both run every day.
- daily-tutoring.yml has its own cron schedule at 0 9 * * * (UTC).
- Result: 2 runs/day for the general scraper + 1 run/day for tutoring = 3 runs/day.
- workflow_dispatch is also enabled, adding manual runs.
Impact
- Over-triggering is explained by deliberate double-cron for DST + separate tutoring workflow.
- This inflates Actions usage and can collide with Groq usage windows.

2) Gross underutilization of the Groq API
Findings
- General pipeline LLM quota is hardcoded at DAILY_LLM_QUOTA = 1500 (jobs/day).
  Tutoring pipeline quota is TUTORING_LLM_QUOTA = 500.
- Incremental scraper skips any job seen in the last 24 hours; cached jobs are not re-analyzed.
- content-hash cache (cache/*.json) bypasses repeated LLM calls across runs.
- BasicRemoteDetector only sends LOW-confidence jobs to the LLM, but most savings come from
  incremental caching rather than the prefilter.
- RemoteOK is pre-classified and skip_analysis=True, but in scrape_with_incremental_quota
  RemoteOK still consumes per-site quota despite not using the LLM.
- The general workflow currently runs the sites: jemepropose, allovoisins, codeur, remoteok.
  Many of these are not remote-first and yield low volumes of LLM-eligible new jobs.
Impact
- Effective Groq usage is capped by: limited daily quota, incremental caching, and the
  remoteok quota “tax” even though it is LLM-free.
- Daily volume of new jobs appears low (site mix + incremental), leading to <1,500 tokens/day
  despite a 300k/day token budget.

3) Tutoring & STEM job focus (priority vertical)
Findings
- There is a dedicated tutoring pipeline (tutoring_scraper.py) and exports
  tutoring_opportunities_latest.json/csv.
- Tutoring pre-filter uses broad tutoring keywords, but there is no explicit STEM focus filter.
- TutoringClassifier does classify subject_category (math_science, technology, etc.),
  but no prioritization or separate queue exists for STEM.
- General pipeline does not implement a tutoring STEM priority queue.
Impact
- Tutoring is separated but STEM priority is not enforced.
- No dedicated export or priority processing for math/physics/chemistry/CS/actuarial science.

4) Scraping process re-evaluation (sources, logic, coverage)
Findings
- Active sources in production are limited to:
  - jemepropose.com (general French services marketplace)
  - allovoisins.com (general French services marketplace)
  - codeur.com (French project marketplace)
  - remoteok.com (remote-only tech jobs via API)
  - tutoring: voscours.fr + findtutors.co.uk + pre-filtered general sites
- Other scrapers (malt, freelance.com, comet) are template selectors and not used in workflows.
- Most sources are France-centric, not Europe/US wide; only remoteok is global.
- No dedicated US/EU remote job boards beyond RemoteOK.
- No deduplication across sources except URL-based history (job_history.json).
Impact
- Coverage is insufficient for the stated goals (EU/US remote at max volume).
- Over-reliance on local services boards yields low remote volume and high on-site noise.

5) Volume & storage maximization
Findings
- Outputs are overwritten daily (jobs_latest.json/csv, remote_jobs_latest.json/csv).
- job_history.json retains URL-level history but is pruned after 30 days.
- Scraper reduces total volume via incremental filtering and conservative quotas.
- RemoteOK jobs count against quota even though LLM is skipped.
Impact
- Storage is not maximizing historical volume (daily overwrite + 30-day history cleanup).
- Total volume is constrained by quotas and limited source breadth.

Summary of what works
- Incremental caching and content-hash cache reduce redundant LLM calls.
- RemoteOK API integration is stable and LLM-free.
- Tutoring pipeline exists and writes a tutoring-specific export.

Summary of what is broken/underperforming
- Over-triggering due to duplicate cron entries + separate tutoring schedule.
- Groq capacity is not used due to quota design, caching, and source mix.
- No STEM priority queue despite classification support.
- Source mix and geography do not match EU/US remote volume goals.
- Storage strategy overwrites daily results and prunes history aggressively.
